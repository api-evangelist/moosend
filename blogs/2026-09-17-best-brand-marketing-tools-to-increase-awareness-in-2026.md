Brand marketing isn’t something you see results from overnight. It’s what makes people think of you when the time comes to buy. But here’s the challenge: there’s no single category called “brand marketing tools.” Instead, brands rely on a combination of platforms to tell their story, build community, monitor conversations, and measure awareness across channels.
